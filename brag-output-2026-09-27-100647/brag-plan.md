# Task Lantern — one clear view

A replacement for the slow first walkthrough: 16 seconds, 1080p landscape, crisp cuts, directed close-ups, minimal captions, original rhythmic music. This video shows the rebuilt product, not a mock UI.

## Product rubric
1. Product: one offline HTML workspace for registered Claude Code/Codex threads across projects.
2. Claim: switch tracked threads and see current progress without losing the page; expand details only when needed.
3. Hook: real product visible immediately, “Your threads. One clear view.”
4. Source: actual browser interaction with the current multi-project demo, clearly marked sample data.
5. Shortest useful story: 16 seconds, no slow cursor tour or long intro.
6. Tone: precise, brisk, warm; purposeful camera framing and readable holds.
7. Audio: original 120 BPM pulse, bass and percussion with small click accents; no voice or sampled music.
8. Post copy: “Task Lantern now puts your tracked coding-agent threads in one local workspace. Switch projects on the right, see progress and decisions at a glance, and expand the details without leaving the page.”
9. Flow: summary → switch projects → decision defaults → expand plan → collapse sidebar/dark mode → GitHub.
